Our Story

Beauty crafted with heart, in the heart of Johannesburg

Christelle Hair Beauty is a South African beauty salon proudly based in Braamfontein, Johannesburg. We specialise in hair styling, hair treatments, braids, hair transformations, wig styling, nails and eyelashes — bringing together technical skill and genuine artistry for every client who sits in our chair.

From protective styles to full hair transformations, every service is delivered with attention to detail, creativity and a warm, welcoming experience that keeps our clients coming back.
